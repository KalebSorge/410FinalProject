Our Mission

Ever since our founding in 1973 as a small hobbyist shop, Van Alden Electronics has been at the forefront of computer engineering and electronic development. From Napa Valley to the current innovations of the scanned brain we strive to make the average man more connected, more powerful, and more capable.

Our Past

Founded by Adam Van Alden in 1973, Van Alden Electronics was initially a small hobby for our founder. Adam mainly tinkered with breakers, and other household aplliances, but gained a love for the fledgling computers of the time. In 1977, Adam was contracted with a few friends to work on the Air Force Strategic Air Command detection systems. In 1982, the Napa Valley Ship incident occured, leading to an explosion in technology as we know it. With Adam at the forefront of this discovery, Van Alden Electronics exploded into the private scene in 1985 creating the first desktop computer, and prototyped the first robot with self aware capabilities only two years later.

Our Present

As of now, Van Alden Electronics supplies varying countries, companies, and households with computers, robotic assistance, and various medical machines. Our notable products may be viewed under our Products tab.

Our Future

With our breakthroughs in Artificial Intellegence and Robotics, our company strives for more innovation. From home appliances, to military, medical. or industrial purposes, Van Alden Electronics is leagues above major competitiors.
